Use WASD to move, Space to jump, Shift to sneak and climb down ladders. Click to mine/place blocks, number keys 1‑9 and 0 or mouse wheel to change selected block.
Hold mouse and look around. Press the backtick (`) key to toggle debug info.
Torches emit light, ladders let you climb, redstone torches power pistons, sticky pistons and TNT. Cacti and sugar cane spawn naturally; touching cacti causes damage. Snow appears in cold biomes, and rain or snow may occur depending on the weather. Sticky pistons push blocks when powered and pull them back when the redstone signal turns off. Falling from a height will injure you. Hostile mobs (zombies, skeletons and creepers) spawn at night: zombies chase you up close, skeletons shoot arrows from a distance, and creepers silently approach and explode when near you. Avoid their blasts and survive as long as you can!
